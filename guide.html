REHEVANE — Ending Guide

Acts I–III complete saga · 12 finale endings + 3 Act I variants. There is no ending picker — thy play decides thy fate, and the finale merely reveals it. Watch it live in ESC → 👑 King's Decree.

1 · How endings are decided (the fate ladder)

Score = bond − jealousy per heroine. The ladder is read top to bottom; the first rung that holds is thy fate:

RungRuleEnding
1 · HouseholdAll six met, all bonds ≥ 6, total jealousy ≤ 10All Six
2 · DevotionTop score ≥ 14 and leads second by ≥ 6Her solo
3 · True pairTop two are a true pair (Ruby+Nyx, Rosalind+Belladonna, Minerva+Daisy), within 5, both bonds ≥ 8Their pair
4 · Truest heartTop score ≥ 8Top solo
5 · SolitudeOtherwiseAlone (Warden's Road)

So: pour everything into one girl → devotion solo. Raise a true pair above the rest → pair. Balance all six and keep jealousy cool → harem. Rush through → alone. Jealous fury drags score down — a furious love cannot win.

Soulbound exception: wake Lyra in the Hidden Grotto (full-moon midnight, before the Hollow's Stag falls) and Solitude leaveth the ladder — the lowest fate becometh Lyra (“Second Heartbeat”), for she dwelleth within thy chest. Her bonds stand maxed from three centuries of watching, and her Vow is available at once.

Act I also has 3 variants (devoted / hasty / open) from thy Oath conduct. Epilogue households gain 1–4 children, random genders (boys-only, girls-only, or mixed) — except Alone.

3 · Jealousy — the anime rules

  • Grand favor (+2 affection at once: gifts, dates, big scenes) for one girl gives +1 jealousy to every other girl who already cares — +2 to Belladonna. Small talk never stings.
  • At 3+ jealousy (Belladonna 2+) she acts: speak with her and she launches a favor-contest scene (spar, interrogation, summons, race, study, cake-off) to win thee back. Winning resets her to 0 — but some other girl saw it (+1 drama).
  • Dates reset HER jealousy to 0.
  • Quiet days cool all hearts −1 (rest at the inn, let time pass). Neglect cools; lavish favor heats.
  • Locked out of a path? The ladder reads score = bond − jealousy: cool her fury (rest, date her, answer her rivalry) or raise her bond, and watch the Decree move.

4 · Efficient path to all endings

  1. Play Acts I–III sincerely (kind/brave answers). Story alone carries every bond to ~7–15.
  2. Talk daily (+1 each), gift spare Moonpetals, date the most jealous girl each evening.
  3. Answer rivalry scenes — they pay bonds AND reset jealousy.
  4. Before the Depths: rest 2–4 quiet days until every jealousy ≤ 3 and total ≤ 12.
  5. Slay the Core, approach the Altar — no menu appears. Thy mornings speak: the threads route thee to the earned ending. Check ESC → 👑 King's Decree beforehand to see exactly where thou standest.
  6. For ONE true love: lavish her (dates, gifts, scenes, her Vow) and let the rest cool — devotion needs a lead of 6.
