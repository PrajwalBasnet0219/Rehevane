REHEVANE — STORY BIBLE
=========================
(Every story the game hath. Current canon. game.txt keepeth the original
novel draft; where they differ, this file ruleth.)

------------------------------------------------------------
1 · THE NAME
------------------------------------------------------------
REHEVANE meaneth a grand fortress built on a high cliff, where magic and
stone become one. Root words in the Old Tongue mean "high cliff" and
"keeper of the light." The place: an ancient castle high above the clouds,
its glowing crystals powering walls that hold back dark creatures. Its
purpose: kings meet there to make peace; wizards study old secrets in the
high towers.

The sky-fallen hero beareth no name of this world. In Act I, the Guardian's
Oath asketh him to speak his own name (the player speaketh it; the Oath
recordeth it). But the world calleth him by title: REHEVANE — "thou art my
fortress," "thou art where I am safe," magic and stone become one, and so
did we. NPCs address him as Rehevane. His own thoughts, portraits and the
fate-tree keep his oath-name.

------------------------------------------------------------
2 · RULE OF SPEECH
------------------------------------------------------------
The hero speaketh modern words — he is from Earth, and modern words mark
him stranger everywhere. The people of Rehevane speak old words:
thou = you, thee = you (as object), thy = your, thine = yours,
art = are, hast = have, hath = has, doth = does, wilt = will,
shalt = shall. Sentences stay short, that old speech readeth easy.

------------------------------------------------------------
3 · THE WORLD AND ITS WOUND
------------------------------------------------------------
Long ago came the First Off-worlder: an Earth man who lost someone and
begged the world to end goodbye — "Make it that none leave me. Make love
last evermore. Make it that I hear farewell never again."

The world obeyed — wrongly, and literally. Love that refuseth loss became
chain, brand, stone and oath. Thus the HEART DEVOURER was born: not hate,
but love that will not face loss. It twisteth love into chains and knives,
seedeth corruption nodes as scar tissue, and hungereth still. Hunger dieth
not in one morn — but it can learn one word: ENOUGH.

The hero is immune to fragments. That maketh him the HEART-BEARER — wanted
by every power: wardens as leash, Silverwall as weapon, the Devourer as heir.
Fragments run cold to his hand and burn all others.

Heart Threads run between all living things — golden, blue, green, fraying
black — and he, thread-seer, beholdeth them at a glance.

------------------------------------------------------------
4 · FACTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
GREENWOOD WARDENS — elves of the Whispering Greenwood. Bows, root-halls,
the great tree Vael-Thir. They leash what they fear: the Guardian's Oath
bindeth a captain's life to her charge — should his being bring ruin, her
heart stoppeth ere his.

SILVERWALL KINGDOM — human walled cities, white stone, blue banners. King
Aldric the Patient. The knight order trains Belladonna. Split within:
isolationists vs interventionists; both want the Heart-Bearer as weapon.

ASHEN SPIRES — dragonkin of ember and crater. Ruby's volcanic throne. Her
family curse: wish for thyself, and thy heart stones. Proud, ceremonial,
rotting from skyfire madness.

PLAINS DEMI-FOLK — wolf-eared packs under the brand law: collar-marks that
force obedience. Nyx chose her own pack — loudly, and in front of witnesses.

MISTVEIL MARSH — one crooked tower, one kettle, ten thousand candles.
Minerva charts the curse alone while her arms darken with it.

STARLIA CHURCH — white stairs, ever-ringing bells. Raised Daisy kindly and
spendeth her freely: thousands of prayers drain through her smile into the
pit behind her heart — the Devourer's chief anchor.

THE DEVOURER — grief with teeth. Scar tissue: wolves, thorns, blades, nodes.
It speaketh with borrowed voices: "don't leave me," "STAY," "NEVER FAREWELL."

5 · DRAMATIS PERSONAE (every name carrieth meaning)
------------------------------------------------------------
ROSALIND ("pretty rose") — pink-haired elf Guard-Captain, red cape, black
bow. TSUNDERE, 27. Past DARK: her mother died trusting a human; the Oath
will stop her own heart if she cares. Arc: oath-bound guard → reluctant
teacher → woman who chooseth trust though the curse punisheth it.

BELLADONNA ("beautiful lady" — and the deadly nightshade) — dark-haired
knight-maid of Silverwall, violet-black gown, purple flower. YANDERE with
dandere lean, 23. Past DARK: orphaned squire, first charge died on her
first road; love unheld is love lost. Arc: perfect shield → Sworn Shield
rite → protective miracle or Devourer's tool, by the player's hands.

RUBY (the red fire-gem) — platinum crowned dragon Princess of the Ashen
Flame, white-red fur gown, low horns. HIMEDERE, 29. Past WHOLESOME: never
loved as Ruby, only as Highness; one selfish dream — a whole sunrise, hand
in hand, crownless. Arc: statue → woman who dareth one sunrise.

NYX (Greek, "night") — dark-violet wolf-eared huntress of the Plains,
amber-red eyes, black-red coat. KUUDERE with sadodere edge, 21. Past CUTE
with dark edge: branded collar-child; chose Rin — chose REHEVANE — loudly.
Arc: barbed distance → chosen vulnerability → free choice.

MINERVA (Roman wisdom) — silver-lavender scholar-witch of the Marsh,
glasses, blue robe. KUUDERE, 26. Past WHOLESOME: trusts footnotes more than
folk; arms darkening; wanteth, unscientific, to be read clearly, leaned
upon, stayed with. Arc: distance → being seen → leaning.

DAISY ("day's eye") — golden Saint of Starlia, blue-white dress. DEREDERE
with hime edge, 24. Past WHOLESOME with dark core: the Church spendeth her;
every prayer draineth through her smile. Smallest wish: one ordinary
morning, loved as just a girl. Arc: symbol → person.

LYRA — silver bound-heart, Keeper of the Sunken Archive, 22. DEREDERE.
Born a bound-spirit, promised to a sky-fallen soul not yet fallen; sealed
300 years in the grotto wall by the Thorned Stag's grief-magic — her spirit
locked within her own sleeping flesh, lest any thread-seer wake her counsel.
Three bindings fell to three truths; the last seal broke to a freely-given
PRINCESS KISS. Now she dwelleth within his chest as his second heartbeat —
guiding, teasing, loving aloud, stepping out in miniature whensoever he
idlest. Bonus thread (the Decree counteth only the six). Soulbloom [F]:
30 to all foes, cooldowns washed clean, forty heartbeats of surge. Full
moon, midnight to one, openeth her falls; the Stag's death sealeth them
forever.

AEGIS (Greek, "shield-ward") — Lyra's seal-warden, a teal warden-light.
Not a spell but a spirit. Passive: +25% XP, +50% spoils, deep mana (8/s),
quarter-ward on wounds. Active [T]: half flesh, full mana, 8-heartbeat
shield, full cleanse, 6-heartbeat auto-volley (45 heartbeats — 30 once Lyra
is vowed). Counsel in Raphael cadence: Notice. Report. Warning.

6 · ACT I — GREENWOOD (Outpost, Forest, Hollow)
------------------------------------------------------------
A Tokyo convenience-store shift endeth in white light: the sky cracketh
violet, and he falleth through light onto damp leaves — into corrupted
wolves, and Rosalind's arrow. "Stay where thou art, human."

The elders smell Devourer on the night and a human from the sky, and would
slay him; Rosalind beareth him instead under the Guardian's Oath — her life
for his leash. He speaketh his name into the Oath and chooseth his past
(class) in-world. He seeth Heart Threads: a pale-green one, shy, from her
to him.

Wolves purged (4). The Hollow's ward-door: the Thorned Stag — grief made
warden, speaking with a man's voice — slain; the fragment burneth wardens
but chill-eth only him. He beareth it to Rosalind. She nameth him
Heart-Bearer and feareth what it meaneth.

After dark, unarmored, unbound hair, east platform: hands, oaths, or teases
— then dawn, the gate road, and Act II. (No interruption: the act turneth
on a toast and walketh on.)

------------------------------------------------------------
7 · ACT II — ASHES, CHAINS, WHISPERS (Gate, Spires, Sky, Plains, Marsh)
------------------------------------------------------------
GATE — Belladonna, ??? Knight no more: "Halt, traveler... Rehevane?"
SPIRES — Ruby's court by day (borrowed words, well spent), balcony by
night; the stone-heart curse and one promised sunrise walk.
SKY — the corrupted Sky-Node vortex, fed on despair; steadied by ruled
flame and fragment-bearing.
PLAINS — Nyx: reavers driven off, brand-threads cut, pack chosen.
MARSH — Minerva: seen clearly, leaned upon; the tower vision of the First
Sin — the First Off-worlder's prayer that made the Devourer. "I shall not
repeat him. I choose, and keep choosing."

------------------------------------------------------------
8 · ACT III — BLADES, SAINTS, CHOICE (Silverwall, Starlia, Depths)
------------------------------------------------------------
SILVERWALL — Belladonna's guard; two Oathless blades felled; the Sworn
Shield rite (halt-word bound); then her fear unraveled on the Walls, oath
for oath, till the hungry thing drew back — waiting.
STARLIA — Daisy, anchor of thousands of prayers; side chapel after dark;
one ordinary morning, selfish and glorious.
DEPTHS — beneath the cathedral: the Devourer struck through love itself,
and each answered in her own tongue — halt, care, sunrise, fang, failure,
maidhood. He bade farewell without letting go of love: "Go — and rest."
Fragments warmed. Threads loosened into choice. The pit learned ENOUGH.

FATES (no picker — play decideth): harem (all met, all bonds 6+, jealousy
calm) → devotion (top score 14+, lead 6) → true pairs (Fire & Fang, Oath &
Shield, Ink & Bells, both 8+) → truest solo (8+) → alone... unless soulbound:
Lyra, evermore. Years-later mornings follow, 1–4 children, genders cast by
fate. The Devourer starved — not died.

------------------------------------------------------------
9 · INTERLUDE — THE SUNKEN ARCHIVE (hidden, Act I grotto)
------------------------------------------------------------
West of the Greenwood, behind falling water that parteth only at full-moon
midnight (twelve to one) — sealed forever if the Stag falleth first. The
wall-script, three truths, and the last seal: no lock of iron, only a
freely-given princess kiss, lip to lip, from the promised heart. She waked,
asked if he meant it, and went within him. Aegis kindled beside. From that
day two lights keep him: teal counsel at his shoulder, silver-red dawn
behind his ribs — counting his heartbeats and calling them hers.

------------------------------------------------------------
10 · LAWS THAT BIND THE TALE
------------------------------------------------------------
- Full moon: day 3, then every 8th; the falls part midnight–1am only.
- The Stag's death ere the waking sealeth the grotto in ALL acts.
- The Oath: his ruin stoppeth her heart ere his.
- Favor caps at 8 per axis; a spoken Devotion (vow) openeth 9–10. Vow
  needeth bond 20+ with trust, respect and loyalty 5+ each. Lyra's heart
  is locked whole from the first — no drama toucheth it.
- Grand favor (+2 affection at once) stingeth every caring heart (+2 to
  Belladonna); jealousy at 3+ (Belladonna 2+) competeth for him.
- Solitude is no fate once soulbound — yet ever a fate if she sleepeth on.

------------------------------------------------------------
11 · OPEN THREADS (Acts IV–VI ground)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Hunger dieth not: the Devourer starved, learning "enough" — what next?
- Scars remain: Oath-scar silent, stone thinned, brand paled, veins clear —
  none fully gone. Belladonna's hunger only drew back, waiting.
- Lyra evermore: a wife within the chest, through every realm to come.
- The fortress itself — REHEVANE on the high cliff — unvisited. Kings,
  peace-talks, high towers of old secrets: all unwritten.
- The First Off-worlder's line: are there more sky-fallen? Who else fell?
